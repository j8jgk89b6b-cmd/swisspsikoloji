Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Website and contact form

The contact form on this website does not send your entries to a Swiss Psikoloji server. It prepares a WhatsApp draft so you can review it and choose whether to send it. If you send the message, WhatsApp/Meta’s terms and privacy practices apply. Submitting the form does not confirm an appointment; the date and time are confirmed separately.

Information requested

The form includes name, phone number, area of interest, optional preferred date/time, and an optional short message. Do not send health details or sensitive personal information that is not necessary for an initial enquiry. This website is not an emergency service.

Local storage and third-party content

Your Turkish/English language preference may be saved in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember your choice. The map section may load Google Maps content. WhatsApp and Google may process technical data under their own privacy policies. The site should not be assumed to use visitor analytics or advertising-tracking cookies; this policy must be updated if the implementation changes.

Security and external links

No internet communication method can be guaranteed absolutely secure. When you open WhatsApp or another external service, that provider’s terms apply. Links to external sites do not mean that Swiss Psikoloji controls their content or practices.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if site features or data practices change. For rights and request procedures relating to personal data, read the Personal Data Information Notice.
