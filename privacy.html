Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Website and Contact Form

Contact form entries are processed by FormSubmit for email delivery; they are not stored in a database on this website. If you choose the WhatsApp link, your message is sent through WhatsApp/Meta. Submitting the form does not confirm an appointment; date and time are confirmed separately.

Information Requested

The form includes name, phone number, area of interest, optional preferred date/time, and an optional short message. Do not send health details or sensitive personal information that is not necessary for an initial enquiry. This website is not an emergency service.

Local Storage and Third-Party Content

Your Turkish/English language preference may be saved in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember your choice. The map section may load Google Maps content. WhatsApp and Google may process technical data under their own privacy policies. This site is intended not to use visitor analytics or advertising-tracking tools; this policy should be updated if the implementation changes.

Security and External Links

No internet communication method can be guaranteed absolutely secure. When you open WhatsApp or another external service, that provider’s terms apply. Links to external sites do not mean that Swiss Psikoloji controls their content or practices.
